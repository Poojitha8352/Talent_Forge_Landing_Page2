How Talent Forge Works

A structured journey from initial enquiry to membership onboarding and project-related communication.

01

Explore Talent Forge

Explore the Talent Forge programme to understand its purpose, the professional categories available and the way membership enquiries are handled. Read the information carefully, identify the category that matches your experience and note any questions you would like to discuss with the team.

02

Select Your Plan

Review the five membership options—Starter, Professional, Specialist, Elite and Auron—along with their displayed fees and listed details. Compare the information with your own goals and budget, and contact the authorised team if you need clarification about benefits, duration or applicable terms before making a decision.

03

Submit Your Enquiry

Complete the enquiry form with accurate contact information, your professional category, skills, experience and preferred membership level. Add a clear message about the kind of work or opportunity you are interested in so the team can understand your requirements and respond appropriately.

04

Associate Discussion

After submitting your enquiry, connect with the authorised Talent Forge associate for a discussion. Use this conversation to clarify the enrolment process, required information, membership conditions, payment details and any other questions; proceed only after you understand and agree to the applicable terms.

05

Profile Review

Provide your updated profile, qualifications, portfolio and relevant work experience for review. The team may consider the information you share alongside available project requirements, skill expectations and timelines; keep your details accurate so that suitability can be assessed appropriately.

06

Project Communication

When relevant project information is available, the team may share details such as the work scope, required skills, expected timelines and coordination process. Review the information, ask questions where needed and confirm your interest and availability before accepting any assignment.

07

Project Execution

If a project is offered and mutually accepted, complete the work according to the agreed scope, quality expectations, deadlines and communication process. Keep the concerned contact updated on progress, raise blockers early and follow the project instructions and agreed responsibilities.

08

Completion & Review

Once the agreed work is completed, submit the required deliverables and participate in the review or feedback process. Any closure, approval and applicable compensation will be handled according to the specific project agreement and confirmed terms.

Talent Forge · Sales Force

Building Better Client Connections

The Sales Force function supports communication between interested professionals and the Talent Forge programme. It focuses on understanding enquiries, explaining available membership information, coordinating follow-ups and helping people reach the appropriate next step.

Sales Overview

A professional, information-led approach to enquiry handling and membership communication.

What this means: The Sales Force is the first point of contact between a person who submits an enquiry and the Talent Forge programme. Its job is to listen, share accurate membership information and guide the person to the correct next step. It does not promise project allocation, income or returns — official terms and written confirmation from Alpha Auron Private Limited always apply.

Understand Enquiries

Understand the prospect's background, skills, interests and reason for contacting Talent Forge.

In detail: When someone submits the enquiry form, the associate reads the details — professional category, skills, experience, current status and chosen plan — so the first conversation is relevant to that person.

Explain Membership

Communicate the available membership levels, stated fees, duration and applicable programme information.

In detail: The associate shares the five membership levels with their stated fees and 6–12 month duration, and makes it clear that project values shown are illustrative and not guaranteed.

Support Next Steps

Help interested individuals understand the enquiry, discussion and enrolment process.

In detail: If the person wishes to continue, the associate explains what happens next — discussion, review of the terms and the official enrolment steps — so no step comes as a surprise.

Our Sales Process

A clear sequence for managing an enquiry from first contact to documented next steps.

How to read this: Every enquiry follows the same eight steps so that nothing is missed. The steps run from receiving the enquiry to ongoing coordination, and each one has a clear purpose: understand the person, explain the programme accurately, answer questions and record the outcome.
STEP 01

Enquiry Received

Receive the prospect's details and understand the purpose of their enquiry.

In detail: This starts automatically when the enquiry form is submitted. The enquiry is saved with a unique Lead ID and the associate is notified by email.

STEP 02

Initial Communication

Contact the prospect through the appropriate channel and understand their requirements.

In detail: The associate reaches out by phone, email or WhatsApp to introduce herself, confirm the details and understand what the person is looking for.

STEP 03

Programme Explanation

Explain the available membership plans, fees, duration and relevant conditions accurately.

In detail: Plans, fees, duration and conditions are explained exactly as published, so the person can compare the five levels using correct information.

STEP 04

Requirement Discussion

Understand professional skills, experience, category and expectations.

In detail: The associate asks about skills, experience and goals to understand which category and membership level matches the person's expectations.

STEP 05

Follow-up

Respond to questions and share verified information requested by the prospect.

In detail: Questions raised in earlier steps are answered, and only verified information is shared. Anything uncertain is checked before a reply is given.

STEP 06

Enrolment Guidance

Explain the official enrolment steps and direct the prospect to the authorised process.

In detail: The person is told the official enrolment steps and directed to the authorised process. Enrolment is completed only through that process.

STEP 07

Status Update

Keep enquiry status and communication records organised for appropriate follow-up.

In detail: After each conversation the enquiry status is updated (for example Contacted, Follow-up or Enrolled) so the records stay accurate.

STEP 08

Ongoing Coordination

Direct membership-related questions to the appropriate team or authorised contact.

In detail: Once the initial process is complete, membership questions are passed to the right team or authorised contact so the person gets a reliable answer.

Lead Management

Organised lead management helps ensure enquiries are recorded, understood and followed up in a consistent manner.

What is a lead? A lead is a person who has shown interest by submitting the enquiry form. Lead management means saving each enquiry securely, checking what the person needs and tracking its status, so the associate can follow up on time.

Lead Capture

Collect relevant prospect information through the enquiry form and record the details accurately.

In detail: Each submission is stored securely with a unique Lead ID, the date and time and the lead source, so no enquiry is lost.

Lead Qualification

Understand each enquiry's category, skills, experience, interest and communication requirements.

In detail: The details are reviewed to understand fit — category, skills, experience and interest — so the follow-up is focused and relevant.

Lead Tracking

Maintain clear enquiry status and communication history to support timely follow-up.

In detail: Each lead carries a status and communication history, so the associate knows who to contact next and when.

Client Relationship Management

Professional communication builds clarity, trust and a better experience throughout the enquiry journey.

Why it matters: This is about how the team communicates. The aim is that every person receives clear, honest and respectful answers, so they can decide for themselves whether the membership suits them.

Understand Client Needs

Listen carefully to the prospect's questions, expectations and professional goals.

In detail: Listening comes first: the associate notes the person's goals and doubts before sharing any information.

Clear Communication

Provide accurate information in a simple, respectful and professional manner.

In detail: Information is shared in simple language, without exaggeration, so the person clearly understands what is and is not included.

Relationship Support

Maintain appropriate follow-up and help prospects reach the right contact for further assistance.

In detail: The team follows up at suitable times and connects the person with the right contact if more help is needed.
